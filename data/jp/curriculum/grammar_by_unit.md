# Kotoba CEFR-Inspired Grammar By Unit

This is the canonical planned course map for frozen Kotoba Japanese units.

Kotoba uses CEFR-inspired / JF-aligned level labels as learner-facing signposts,
not as certification claims. Level boundaries are defined by Can-do outcomes:
what a learner can understand and produce with controlled Japanese sentence
streams. Grammar is the support structure for those outcomes.

Each authored unit should still follow the core Kotoba rule: introduce new
vocabulary first with familiar grammar, then bring in the unit's new grammar
focus late, after the sentence world has enough usable words.

## A1: Survival Foundations

Can do: identify people, things, and places; ask simple questions; describe
static scenes; talk about simple past and time.

Units 1-7 include two real verb `newWords` each, drilled through short `Vます`
action sentences while the main grammar remains simple. Units 8-14 may include
one or two fixed `Vます` action-preview cards that reuse those known verb
identities. Unit 21 still introduces polite verbs as a productive system.

001. First Sentences
    - `Aです`
    - `Aですか`
    - early `AはBです` preview

002. Talking About Things
    - `AはBです`
    - `AはBですか`
    - `AとBはCです`
    - `AかBかCですか`

003. People And Possession
    - `AのB`
    - `AもBです`

004. Saying What It Is Not
    - `Aではありません`
    - `Aじゃありません`

005. Talking About The Past
    - `Aでした`
    - `Aではありませんでした`

006. This And That
    - `これ` / `それ` / `あれ`
    - `このN` / `そのN` / `あのN`

007. Asking What And Who
    - `何`
    - `誰`
    - `どれ`
    - `どのN`

008. Asking Where And When
    - `どこ`
    - `いつ`
    - simple place and time questions

009. Sentence Endings
    - `ね`
    - `よ`

010. I-Adjectives, Present
    - `大きいN`
    - `Nは大きいです`

011. I-Adjectives, Negative And Past
    - `大きくないです`
    - `大きかったです`
    - `大きくなかったです`

012. Na-Adjectives, Present
    - `静かなN`
    - `Nは静かです`

    - `静かではありません`
    - `静かでした`
    - `静かではありませんでした`

014. Degree Words
    - `とても`
    - `あまり...ない`

015. Existence For Things
    - `Nがあります`

016. Existence For People And Animals
    - `Nがいます`

017. Existence In A Place
    - `場所にNがあります`
    - `場所にNがいます`

018. Location Words
    - `上` / `下` / `中`
    - `前` / `後ろ` / `隣`
    - `Aの上`

019. Basic Numbers And Counters
    - `一つ` / `二つ` / `三つ`
    - `Nが一つあります`

020. A1 Scene Review
    - mixed identity, possession, question, time, and location sentences
    - no new core grammar
