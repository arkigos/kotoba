# Kana builder and local sans typography

The Next learner replaces Instrument Serif with a consistent humanist sans
system. Display text uses `--font-display` (Segoe UI Variable Display, Aptos
Display, Avenir Next, Segoe UI, then the system font); controls and body text use
the matching `--font-body` stack. Japanese uses `--font-japanese`, with installed
Hiragino, Yu Gothic UI, or Meiryo before the system fallback. Display text has
medium or semibold weight. The app no longer imports Google Fonts, so navigation,
lesson text, and activity screens do not wait for a remote font request.

New styles should use these tokens rather than naming a separate display font.
The paper palette and watercolor assets remain the visual accent; headings use
the same readable sans vocabulary as the controls.

## Kana builder

`GamesView.tsx` offers a fourth activity, stored as activity kind `reading`.
Learners see a kanji word and its English meaning, then assemble its existing
kana reading from shuffled tiles. The shared word pool and canonical learning
IDs apply, including priority words and saved dictionary entries.

- Questions require a kanji surface and an unambiguous kana-only reading of
  2–8 tiles. Kana-only words are excluded because their surface reveals the
  answer. A reading with only one distinct tile is also excluded.
- Small kana stay attached to the preceding sound. The small `っ`, nasal `ん`,
  and vowel-length mark `ー` remain separate tiles. Repeated sounds retain
  separate selectable tiles. Shuffling never starts with the complete answer.
- Learners tap tiles or press 1–8, undo with Backspace, and check or continue
  with Enter. Native button keyboard controls remain available. Tiles and
  controls have at least 44-pixel touch targets and wrap on narrow screens.
- Only a correct first attempt earns accuracy credit. Incorrect checks remain
  in the retry list even after a correction. Showing the reading also counts as
  a missed word; clearing or undoing an unchecked answer does not.
- Completed rounds record distinct words and feed existing activity/review
  metrics. Abandoned rounds do not record completion. Retries contain exactly
  the missed reading words, without adding unrelated targets.
- Audio is optional and is never required to answer. Existing exact word audio
  or device speech is available after checking or revealing a reading. Pause,
  navigation, question advance, and unmount cancel playback. No paid audio is
  generated by this feature.

This activity practices word readings. Its recognition and recall results do
not establish grammar mastery, conversational ability, or CEFR completion.

Focused verification: `npm run next:test -- apps/learner-next/tests/games.test.tsx`
checks exact tile construction, first-attempt scoring, reveal/retry behavior,
keyboard and pause handling, audio cancellation, and existing games.
