# Independent B1 tracks

The October 1 independent-track direction now applies to the authored B1 course.
Completing every A1 and A2 lesson opens all eight B1 tracks. Each track keeps its
own ordered lessons and recalls. There is no B1 starter suite. This supersedes
the interim B1 linear gating described in the A2 decision.

The current B1 course contains 947 instructional/account lessons, 1,003 fixed
recalls, 7,114 original cards and 14,247 placements. Its 2,319 authored identities
are unique, but the bounded B1 pool contains 3,004 words. The remaining 685
words still need authored instruction and review. Finishing the available
material must not mark B1 complete or open an unauthored B2 course.

`scripts/lib/curated-b1-tracks.mjs` explicitly assigns all 36 source chapters and
35 additional grammar constructions. Daily tasks owns practical conditions,
preparation, arrangements, excess and related descriptions. Travel owns restriction
with だけ, variation with によって, and means with による. Work owns activity 中,
roles with として, deadlines, reported advice, and workplace arrangements.
People owns respectful presence and precise event timing. Culture owns definitions
and subject の inside noun descriptions. Public life owns source-attributed
reports, hearsay, and explanatory ので. Science owns measurable adjective nouns.
Health/activity owns advice and descriptions of repeated or developing change.

All completed A1/A2 grammar is shared. Additional B1 grammar may recur only in
its owner until the next level. Every owned pattern has instruction before use,
at least two distinct original contexts, and at least three lesson appearances
and six card placements. Fixed word recalls and 38 grammar checkpoints revisit
earlier material. Eight additional checkpoints preserve the explicitly taught
lexical variants ご主人, お見舞い, 非常に and formal 私（わたくし）.
