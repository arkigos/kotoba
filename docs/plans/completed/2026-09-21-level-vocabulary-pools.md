# Bounded A1–C2 vocabulary pools

User requests explicit level budgets and complete pools, with lower priority for
redundant katakana loans while retaining everyday essentials.

- Audit canonical entries, frequencies, authored vocabulary, and source restrictions.
- Build reproducible, disjoint pools: A1 750; A2 1,250; B1 3,000; B2 5,000;
  C1 7,000; C2 8,000 (25,000 cumulative).
- Author early-level selection and loanword exceptions/preferences; distinguish
  curriculum membership from historical course placements and reference estimates.
- Export all selections with evidence and review samples personally; iterate on
  noise, duplicates, misplaced everyday vocabulary, and loanword concentration.
- Expose pools in dictionary browsing without granting sentence-generation support
  or practice credit. Preserve stable IDs and frozen lessons.
- Validate counts, disjoint identity, essential words, spelling/sense restrictions,
  deterministic rebuild, browsing, and builds. Document measured limitations.

Budgets are product scope decisions, not scientifically established Japanese CEFR
word thresholds. The JF Standard defines levels through communicative tasks:
https://www.jfstandard.jpf.go.jp/summaryen/ja/render.do

Completed 2026-09-21. All pools are populated and browseable. Reviewed full A1
and later-level samples, corrected demonstrated defects, and retained provisional
labels for later levels. See `docs/reviews/2026-09-21-level-pools/REPORT.md` for
measured results, verification, and remaining limits.
