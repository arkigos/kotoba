# Dictionary placement, due vocabulary and reusable grammar

User direction: on-demand lessons combine selected new words, due review words,
and practiced helpers only. Grammar has its own learning/review history and becomes
available, with a preference for due grammar, in future generated lessons.

1. Audit the entire pinned dictionary; preserve stable identities, original senses,
   aliases, learning history and audio. Tag every lexical entry A1–C2 using reviewed
   placement first and transparently estimated Kotoba placement otherwise. Keep
   provenance/confidence, deterministic rebuilds, full coverage checks and a manual
   sample review. Clean demonstrated form/reading/gloss defects without deleting
   legitimate homonyms or rare entries.
2. Integrate levels in dictionary browsing/details and verify real UI filters.
3. Audit due-word admission/selection and add tests for unpracticed priorities,
   aliases, preview versus actual practice, and mixed new/review lessons.
4. Add a reviewed grammar catalog, independent lessons and spaced history. Start
   with wanting objects, wanting to do actions, and ability. Known grammar is
   reusable in future lessons; due grammar gets explicit practice. Never infer
   grammar knowledge from vocabulary level or previews.
5. Generate multiple mixed and grammar lessons, read every card personally, repair
   shared engine defects, regenerate and repeat until a complete batch passes.
   Run full tests, both builds and relevant audits. Write the evidence and limits.
