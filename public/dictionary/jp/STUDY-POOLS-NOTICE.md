# Kotoba study pools

The 25,000-entry study index is a selected, ranked derivative of the dictionary
described in [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md). JMdict-derived dictionary data and
wordfreq-derived frequency metadata remain CC BY-SA 4.0. Kotoba adds editorial
membership, display choices, ranks, and introductory stages.

Additional ordering evidence comes from Jamie Sinclair's
[open-anki-jlpt-decks](https://github.com/jamsinclair/open-anki-jlpt-decks), revision
`1ad66734417aca9dbcca6b2d5ee440cb13ab3ba0`. Its MIT license is reproduced in
[OPEN-ANKI-JLPT-LICENSE.txt](OPEN-ANKI-JLPT-LICENSE.txt). The source project credits
chyyran/jlpt-anki-decks and tanos.co.uk for earlier vocabulary lists. Source files,
original README, and checksums are retained in Kotoba's source distribution at
`data/jp/dictionary/upstream/open-anki-jlpt/`.

Community JLPT labels provide relative ordering evidence, not official lists or
a JLPT-to-CEFR conversion. A1 is explicitly selected and reviewed in development;
A2–C2 are provisional ranked selections with editorial corrections. The budgets
are Kotoba product choices, not official CEFR vocabulary requirements. Commonness
and curriculum placement are separate labels. Katakana is a writing proxy, not
a verified foreign-origin classification.

Pool membership does not establish learner knowledge, mastery, or sentence
generation support. Existing lessons and practice history are independent.
